Rest Day
Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday are full rest days.

Muscles grow during recovery, not during the workout itself. Light walking, stretching or mobility is fine — just don't add another session thinking it'll speed things up.

Sleep 7–9 hrs · eat ~0.7–1g protein per lb of bodyweight · show up fresh.
